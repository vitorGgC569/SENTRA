# Third-party notices and component provenance

SENTRA includes third-party software. Product-facing documentation uses SENTRA
terminology; this file preserves attribution and links to the original notices.
These notices do not grant the components execution authority or certify the
complete SENTRA product.

## Workspace and mobile components

Selected components were adapted from ORCA, published by stablyai/Lovecast Inc.
under the MIT license. Copyright and permission notices remain in
[the upstream license](vendor/orca/LICENSE), the integration directories and
packaged dependency notices. Pinned revisions and source inventories remain in
[the component directory](vendor/orca/).

## Desktop presentation components

Selected presentation modules were adapted from Coucou by Louis-CFM under the
MIT license. Their license, origin metadata and preserved upstream module hashes
remain in [the desktop component directory](sentra_canvas/desktop_ui/src/vendor/coucou/).
The installed display includes its third-party notices.

## Engineering records

Historical integration studies and the unedited originals of consolidated notes
are retained in the [component provenance index](vendor/component-records/index.json).
Each entry records its original location, archived location and SHA-256. These
records preserve source references and earlier acceptance limits; they are not
the current product status. See [release readiness](docs/RELEASE_READINESS_2026-10-24.md).

Other dependency licenses remain alongside their components and in generated
distribution notices. This document does not replace those license texts.
