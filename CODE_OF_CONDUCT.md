# Code of Conduct

We want SENTRA to be welcoming and constructive. Treat people with respect, welcome different levels of experience, give actionable feedback, and respect others' privacy. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats and unwanted publication of private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove content or limit participation to protect the community. Report concerns privately to [@vitorGgC569](https://github.com/vitorGgC569), without posting sensitive information in a public issue. Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical.
